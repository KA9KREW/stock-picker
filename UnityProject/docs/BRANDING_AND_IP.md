# Branding & IP guardrails (read before Play Store submission)

## Product positioning

This codebase is an **original game** *inspired by* public-domain / widely documented **Stock Ticker** *board game mechanics* (dice-driven commodity prices, dividends, splits, wipeouts, trading windows). It is **not** a licensed digital adaptation of any specific publisher’s board game.

## Do / Don’t

**Do**

- Use a **new product name**, logo, UI skin, color palette, typography, and sound design.
- Treat mechanics as **generic economic simulation** (dice + commodities + market events).
- Keep third-party licenses and attributions current (`Assets/ThirdParty/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`).

**Don’t**

- Don’t reuse **trademarked titles**, box art, rulebook text, or distinctive trade dress from any publisher.
- Don’t claim “official”, “authorized”, or “licensed” unless you have written rights.

## Google Play practical notes

- Use your own **package name** and branding in **Player Settings**.
- If you add analytics/ads later, update **Data safety** accordingly (current MVP is offline-first).

## Not legal advice

This document is engineering hygiene, not legal counsel. If you are unsure about naming/marketing, consult an attorney.
